# Gameplay Bugs — Community Workarounds

## Weapons & Equipment

### AR-59 Suppressor
**Bug:** Short mag buff not applied, still at pre-patch values.
**Workaround:** None — wait for Arrowhead patch.

### Lumberer's Flamethrower
**Bug:** Not applying damage correctly.
**Workaround:** None confirmed — report to Arrowhead.

### LAS-13 Trident
**Bug:** Increased recoil vs previous patch.
**Workaround:** Adjust aim, use stabilizing perks.

### R-4 Hyena
**Bug:** Loses mags if interrupted during reload.
**Workaround:** Don't interrupt reload; wait for completion.

### Eagle Gas Airstrike
**Bug:** 50 demolition strength (higher than intended).
**Workaround:** None — this may be intentional, report if problematic.

### MGX-42 Bullet Storm
**Bug:** Muzzle flash misaligned.
**Workaround:** Visual only — doesn't affect gameplay.

## Missions & AI

### Stratagem Call-in Times
**Bug:** UI shows wrong call-in time, actual time is correct.
**Workaround:** Ignore UI display; actual times are unaffected.

### Wrong Mission Type
**Bug:** Different mission generated than selected.
**Workaround:** Restart mission selection; report if persistent.

### Automaton Fabricator
**Bug:** Helldiver dies after landing on fabricator at high latency.
**Workaround:** Avoid landing on fabricators; improve connection.

### Evacuate Citizens
**Bug:** Citizen NPCs don't pathfind correctly.
**Workaround:** Lead citizens manually; report pathfinding issues.

## Visual & UI

### Helmet Disappearance (IX-Voidwalker)
**Bug:** Helmet disappears at certain camera angles.
**Workaround:** None — cosmetic only.

### Oil/Dirt/Blood Stains
**Bug:** Armor/helmet turns jet black from effects.
**Workaround:** Clean suit at resupply pod.

### Super Destroyer "Operated By"
**Bug:** Not showing player nickname.
**Workaround:** None — cosmetic only.

### Points of Interest
**Bug:** Don't grey out on mini-map when found.
**Workaround:** None — cosmetic only.

### Red Beacon Stratagems
**Bug:** Don't follow beacon when location changes.
**Workaround:** Don't rely on beacon tracking; use visual cues.
