# Known Issues — Arrowhead Official

Source: [Arrowhead Zendesk](https://arrowhead.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15916898652700--HELLDIVERS-2-Known-Issues)

> This list contains the most common issues reported by players or found in QA.
> It is kept to known, confirmed issues due to be fixed rather than design and balance choices.
> **This is not an exhaustive list of all issues we are aware of.**

## New (latest patch)

- Enemies will target unmanned FRVs
- AR-59 Suppressor short mag buff didn't go through, still at pre-patch values
- New Illuminate enemies clipping through ground/obstacles on High Value Assets missions
- LAS-13 Trident has increased recoil compared to previous patch
- Operating map inside vehicle may cause unintended stim usage
- Lumberer's Flamethrower not applying damage correctly
- IX-Voidwalker helmet disappears at certain camera angles
- Eagle Gas Airstrike has 50 demolition strength (higher than intended)

## Technical

- Microsoft IME languages cannot type Korean/Japanese/Chinese symbols in chat
- Chinese/Japanese IME prevents movement and controls in-game
- Helldiver may die after landing on Automaton fabricator at high latency
- Campaigns menu UI slightly cut off on some resolutions

## Gameplay

- Stratagem weapons show wrong call-in time on UI (actual time unaffected)
- Different mission type generated than selected
- R-4 Hyena loses mags if interrupted during reload
- Ice flowers cause ragdolling when triggered
- Explosives cause Helldivers hidden behind terrain to ragdoll
- Attachment stats not changed in preview until purchasing/equipping
- Melee kills not counted in end-of-mission or career stats
- Oil/dirt/blood visual effects stain armor/helmet jet black
- "Operated By" text under Super Destroyer not showing player nickname
- Points of interest do not grey out on mini-map when found in colony
- Hellpod Avoidance Areas inconsistent with player POV
- Red Beacon Stratagems do not follow beacon when location changes
- MGX-42 Bullet Storm muzzle flash misaligned
- Trees low-quality material on Xbox
- Citizen NPCs in Evacuate Citizens missions may not pathfind correctly
